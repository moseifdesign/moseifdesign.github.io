Before we start.

What does a revision include?

Two rounds of consolidated feedback per thumbnail, refining the agreed direction. A new concept, new brief or additional variations are quoted separately before I continue.

What will I receive?

Final upload-ready PNG/JPG files. Editable PSD files are optional and priced separately before booking; third-party asset licensing may limit what can be transferred.

How does the schedule work?

A first draft typically takes 1–3 days after your brief is complete and your start date is confirmed. Batch and monthly work is scheduled at up to 3 thumbnails per week; revisions and feedback can affect final delivery. Unused monthly slots and any rollover are agreed in writing before booking.

Do you guarantee CTR or views?

No. A thumbnail is one part of video performance, alongside the topic, title, audience and distribution. My focus is clear, compelling design; performance claims need real channel data.
